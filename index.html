Step by step

Getting Around Japan

🚄 How Japanese trains work: 6 things to know
  1. Tickets are small paper cards. Push them into the slot of the gate, walk through, and take them back from the slot on the other side. If you have 2 cards for one trip, put both in together.
  2. The bullet train has its own second gate inside the station (the Shinkansen gate). So at Ueno you go through two gates.
  3. Your ticket tells you where to stand. It shows the car (号車) and seat, for example 7号車 12番 A. On the platform floor, car numbers are painted where the doors will open. Stand on your number.
  4. Trains leave on the exact minute. Be on the platform 10 minutes early. Doors close about 30 seconds before departure and no one waits.
  5. Kagayaki is all reserved. Sit only in your seat. Get off when the screen and English announcement say Kanazawa.
  6. Keep the Dec 8 tickets separate. The machine at Ueno prints both trips. Put the Dec 8 cards inside a passport so they never go into a gate early.
🧳 Your suitcase on the bullet train

No extra booking needed. Put it in the space behind the last row of seats in your car; board as soon as the doors open. Small bags go on the rack above.

Dec 3 · Narita → Ueno

Skyliner · ~45 min · Klook QR
1

After you collect your suitcase

  1. Walk out into the arrivals hall. Look up for the signs Railways / 鉄道 and go down to B1.
  2. Find the blue Keisei counter (not the red-and-green JR one). Show your Klook Skyliner QR and say: "Skyliner to Ueno, two adults." They give you seat tickets.
  3. Insert your ticket(s) at the Keisei gate and take them back.
  4. Your platform says Skyliner. Stand at the car number printed on the ticket.
2

On the Skyliner

  1. Suitcase goes in the luggage space by the doors.
  2. Stay on until the last stop, Keisei Ueno. Do not get off at Nippori.
  3. Insert the tickets at the exit gate (it keeps them). Go up to the street. The APA is across from the station.

Dec 4 · Ueno → Kanazawa

Kagayaki 509 · 10:28 AM · Track 19 to 22, basement 4
1

Walk to JR Ueno · 9:45 AM

  1. From the APA, walk 5 minutes to JR Ueno, Hirokoji exit (広小路口). JR is the big station, not Keisei.
  2. Inside, look for the green machines marked Reserved seat tickets / 指定席券売機, near the Central gate (中央改札).
2

Print your tickets

  1. Tap English, then the pickup option. Scan the QR code from your Klook ticket (Klook emails the exact pickup steps when the ticket is issued).
  2. It prints the tickets for both Dec 4 and Dec 8. Check the dates. Put the Dec 8 ones away in a passport.
  3. Stuck? Go to the staffed JR ticket office next to it and show the Klook ticket.
3

Gate 1: the Central gate

  1. Insert all of today's tickets together, walk through, take them all back.
  2. Inside, buy ekiben (train bento) and coffee at the shops.
4

Gate 2: the Shinkansen gate

  1. Follow the green Shinkansen / 新幹線 signs. You reach a second row of gates. Insert the same tickets again, take them back.
  2. Take the long escalators down to B4. Look at the board for Kagayaki 509 / かがやき 509 · 10:28 and its track number (19 to 22). The board may show a later end station like Tsuruga; that is fine, it stops at Kanazawa.
5

On the platform · by 10:15 AM

  1. Walk to the car number on your ticket (painted on the floor). Wait in line.
  2. Board, put the suitcase behind the last row, find your seats (letters A to E above the windows).
  3. Eat, rest. The ride is 2 hours 21 minutes. Set a phone alarm for 12:40 PM.
6

Arrive Kanazawa · 12:49 PM

  1. Follow the yellow Exit / 出口 signs. Insert today's tickets at the gate. It keeps them, that's normal.
  2. Follow signs for the East exit · Kenrokuen exit (東口 · 兼六園口). You come out at the big wooden Tsuzumi-mon gate.
  3. The taxi line is right in front. Show ホテルインターゲート金沢.

Taxis in Kanazawa

Your main way around · 5 to 7 min almost everywhere
  1. Wait at a taxi stand, or wave one with a red light on the windshield (空車 means free).
  2. The back left door opens and closes by itself. Don't touch it.
  3. Show the Japanese name on your phone. Every stop in this guide has it.
  4. Pay at the end. Many taxis take cards; keep ¥2,000 (₱820) cash ready just in case. No tipping.

Dec 6 · Bus to Shirakawa-go

West exit · Stop 4 · QR ticket
  1. Taxi from the hotel. Show 金沢駅 西口 (Kanazawa Station West exit). This is the opposite side from the wooden gate.
  2. Find Bus Stop 4. Be there 15 minutes early.
  3. Show the Japan Bus Online QR on your phone to the driver. If the QR shows seat numbers, sit there.
  4. Get off at Shirakawa-go (白川郷), the bus terminal where everyone gets off.
  5. Going home: the same terminal. Be there 15 minutes before your bus. You arrive back at the West exit. Take a taxi from the stand there to the hotel.

Dec 8 · Kanazawa → Ueno → Narita

Kagayaki 534 · 1:50 PM · Skyliner
1

At Kanazawa Station · 1:30 PM

  1. Take out the Dec 8 tickets from the passport.
  2. Follow the Hokuriku Shinkansen / 北陸新幹線 signs to the Shinkansen gate. Insert, take back.
  3. Find Kagayaki 534 / かがやき 534 · Tokyo · 13:50 on the board and go to that track. Stand on your car number.
2

Get off at Ueno · 4:14 PM

  1. The train goes on to Tokyo. Ueno is the stop before Tokyo. Get ready when the screen says Ueno. Set an alarm for 4:05 PM.
  2. Follow Exit signs up, through the gates (insert tickets, they keep them).
  3. Follow signs for the Shinobazu exit (不忍口) and Keisei Line. Cross the street: Keisei Ueno is the station on the other side, about 7 minutes.
3

Skyliner to the airport

  1. Blue Keisei counter: show the Klook Skyliner QR, "Skyliner to Narita Airport, two adults."
  2. Get off at Narita Airport Terminal 2·3 (about 45 min). This is the stop before the last one.
  3. Eat at Terminal 2, then the free Toyoko Inn shuttle: Central exit, 1F, Bus Stop 31-B, just past the taxi stand.
